## For Alex to decide: the economy has a dominant strategy (N2)

| Animal | Points | Price at the floor | Points per $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cat | 10 | $1 | 10 |
| Rhino | 35 | $2 | 17.5 |
| **Orca** (XL) | **55** | **$3** | **18.3** |
| **Elephant** (XL) | **60** | **$4** | 15 |

- **Orca + Mammoth + $1 of fuel = $7 for 55 points**, every mission. Elephant + Titan = $9 for 60. The Captain gets $10 each mission, and unspent money carries over, so **money never forces a trade-off.**
- The bots pick animals by rotation and average about 30 points per mission. A team that waits about 90 seconds for the floor and always goes XL roughly doubles that.
- The only things holding XL back are the auction (one listing that jumps back to full price) and fuel time (110 for the Mammoth, about 2 minutes solo). Both shrink once a team knows the trick.

Options, roughly from smallest change to biggest:

1. **Less money:** $7 per mission instead of $10. XL becomes a real stretch, and carryover becomes a decision ("save for an Elephant next mission?").
2. **Higher floors for big items:** floor at 0.7× instead of 0.5×. The Elephant floor goes from $4 to $5, the Orca from $3 to $4.
3. **Cap carryover** at, say, $5, so money can't pile up.
4. **Points that grow more slowly with size,** or XL that needs noticeably more fuel.

Suggestion: playtest once with real people **as is** (communication may be the real limit for humans), then try option 1. It's a one-number change in the host's lobby settings (Money per mission = 7), so you can try it tonight without a code change.
