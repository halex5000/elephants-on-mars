# Elephants on Mars · host sheet

**25 minutes • 1 host + 4–8 players • 2 missions • standard single listings**

**Before the call.** If deploying, use **Actions → Deploy to Fly → Run workflow** on green main. Type `deploy`; confirm success and one machine. Open a practice room and click **Open big screen**. On a phone with Wi-Fi off, scan the QR and check that your name appears. Create a fresh room. Prepare one breakout per four players.

| Phase | Say and do |
| --- | --- |
| Lobby · 3 min | Share the big-screen tab. Paste the join link in chat. “Join by name. Everyone holds a different piece of the plan.” Set **4 minutes / $10 / 4 per team / 2 missions**. Click **Make teams**. |
| Teams · 2 min | Match breakouts to the game’s rosters. “Introduce your role. What do you know, and who needs to hear it?” Check crew audio. |
| Missions · 4 min each | Click **Start mission 1**. “Captain, share the money. Vet and Pilot, agree on a payload that fits. Engineer, buy and load fuel. Captain, launch before zero.” Let time expire or show results once everyone launches. |
| Results · 2 min | Regroup. “What landed or stayed behind? Which decision took longest?” Record one example. Reopen breakouts and start mission 2. “Check your new role.” |
| Final · 5 min | Click **Show the final report** and **Copy results**. Use the game’s debrief: Who felt in the dark? When did communication click? How did markets change your plan? Who held money or information, and why? What habit would you bring to work? |

**Late arrival:** Have them join, click **Add them to teams**, then move them into the assigned breakout. **Dropped player:** Refresh the original tab. If it closed, have them rejoin and read you their join ID on the call. Select their **Offline seat** and **Returning player**, then click **Restore seat** before adding latecomers. If you use **+1 minute**, record the extension for all crews.

**Observation slip · one per crew per round.** Ask the Engineer to note the clock at first fuel attempt ___ and first successful buy ___ (or *still waiting / never attempted / used stock*). Ask the Pilot for rejected buys ___. At zero: missing animal / rocket / fuel? ___ Landed? ___ Money moved from ___ to ___ because ___. Confusing moment or quote ___.

**Feedback · paste in chat.** ① Which role was hardest to understand? ② What did you wait for, and how long? ③ Why did you keep or switch targets? ④ When did you need money, and who had it? ⑤ What would improve the next round?

| What repeats across crews? | Next playtest decision |
| --- | --- |
| Affordable listings reset before buyers can act; crews miss launch. | Trial `EXTRA_LISTING_MIN_TEAMS=26` in a 26+ team rehearsal. |
| Funded buyers still cannot afford a fitting pair as prices fall. | Trial a lower price on the specific animal or rocket. |
| Funded Engineers know their requirement but wait over a minute for the pool to fill. | Trial faster fuel refill. Separate pool wait from time spent loading. |
| Switching targets or moving a dollar saves launches and feels satisfying. | Keep the current rules. |
